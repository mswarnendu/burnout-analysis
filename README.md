# Burnout Analysis in Tech Jobs

## Background

Burnout is something that has been experienced by most people worldwide, whether that be from a job, from school, from a relationship, or more. Based on this, I wanted to analyze the empirical rates of burnout among different tech jobs.

## Experiment

The dataset I based this off of is a small dataset from Kaggle, and for the sake of simplicity, I just used the burnout_score and job_role columns and checked if the score was 5 or higher. With that info, I'd find the emperical burnout rate with the amount of scores 5 or higher per job and divide that by the total amount of entries in that job. 


## Results

The job with the highest burnout rate is QA Engineering, with a rate of a burnout score over 5 of 56.89%.
However, most of the burnout rates were in this similar range, due to the small dataset.
